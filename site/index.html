THE RULE

Every platform holds you exactly once. The moment you leave it, it cracks and drops into the dark below.

You are a ball that never stops bouncing. You only choose which way it leans — tap the left half of the screen to drift left, the right half to drift right, let go to stop. That is the whole game. No buttons, no joystick, nothing to memorize.

There is no going back and no resting, and a rising darkness follows you up. Fall too far beneath your best height and it takes you.
